About me

Hi, I'm Maksymilian.

MaksForge is where I build the small tools I wish existed. Each one solves a single problem and never asks for an account or shows an ad. The Android apps also work without an internet connection.

Every app is free. If one of them makes your day a little easier, a tip helps me keep building, but it never unlocks anything.
